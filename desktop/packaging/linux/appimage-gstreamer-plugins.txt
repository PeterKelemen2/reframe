# The GStreamer plugins that the AppImage carries, for WebKitGTK's <video>: its player, the
# Matroska and MP4 demuxers and the decoders of GStreamer's core, base and good sets. One plugin
# per line, the name of its file without `libgst` and `.so`. build-in-container.sh copies them
# from the build image; the packages they come from are in appimage-packages.txt.
# No FFmpeg: gst-libav links FFmpeg's libraries, which no package may contain.
